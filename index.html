About Satish

Satish Amancharla

A people-and-change leader who connects design, manufacturing, quality and IT.

Satish is a quality, operations and manufacturing leader with 25+ years in FDA-regulated medical devices — Inspire Medical Systems, Medtronic, St. Jude Medical and Transoma Medical — and in technology and industrial products at HID Global, Transition Networks and Nilfisk. Most recently, as Director of Quality at Inspire Medical Systems, he built the Design Assurance team and grew the complaint-handling organization from infancy to maturity and managed global post-market surveillance as a Class III implantable therapy scaled.

He has spent his career as the bridge between the production floor, the quality system and the data behind them — pairing regulatory discipline with hands-on Python, Oracle and AI development skills. He has led Armchair Technologies since 2012, helping medical device startups launch products and stand up PLM infrastructure, and today brings that same blend of strategy and care to fractional, full-time and interim senior leadership.
