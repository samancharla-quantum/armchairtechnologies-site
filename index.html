About Satish

Satish Amancharla

A people-and-change leader who connects engineering, manufacturing, quality, supply chain and customer-facing teams.

Satish is a cross-functional leader with 25+ years of broad experience across manufacturing, quality, operations, design transfer and NPI, and customer-facing functions in FDA-regulated and ISO manufacturing — in medical devices at Inspire Medical Systems, Medtronic, St. Jude Medical and Transoma Medical, and in technology and industrial products at HID Global, Transition Networks and Nilfisk. Much of his career has centered on design transfer and new product introduction (NPI): moving products from development into manufacturing, through launch and into scale-up. Most recently, as Director of Quality at Inspire Medical Systems, he built the Design Assurance team, grew complaint handling, patient support and customer service as volume scaled from about 1,000 to 150,000+ implants with 66% faster resolution, and managed global post-market surveillance for a Class III implantable therapy.

With roots in electrical and software engineering, he has spent his career as the bridge between the production floor, the quality system and the teams who design, build and support the product, and he now uses that breadth to help organizations work cross-functionally. He has led Armchair Technologies since 2012, helping medical device startups launch products and stand up PLM infrastructure, and today brings that same blend of strategy and care to senior, fractional and interim leadership roles and focused consulting.
